HUMAN DECISION

The scheduler selects the operational response.

The optimisation model can identify plans that satisfy the constraints represented in the model. The scheduler still decides which plan is operationally acceptable.

01 — EXAMPLE BASELINE

Vessel B is planned on Berth 2 from 10:00–14:00.

Vessel D is planned on the same berth from 15:00. This is the simplified baseline used only for this demonstration.

02 — EXAMPLE DISRUPTION

Vessel B is now expected four hours late.

If its four-hour service duration is unchanged, its berth use moves to approximately 14:00–18:00.

03 — RESCHEDULING REQUIRED

The delayed berth schedule conflicts with Vessel D.

With Vessel B shifted to approximately 14:00–18:00, Berth 2 is double-booked from 15:00. A revised berth schedule is required.

04 — CANDIDATE REVISED SCHEDULES

Three revised berth schedules are tested.

Hold B until D clears, prioritise B and delay D, or move B to Berth 1 after Vessel C.

05 — ADD OPERATIONAL CONTEXT

The scheduler can refine the rescheduling task.

The scheduler adds operational knowledge or a rescheduling instruction in natural language. The conversational interface translates that instruction into explicit schedule changes.

06 — CONFIRM INTERPRETATION

The scheduler confirms what PortOptimiser understood.

The interpreted berth, timing and “keep unchanged” requirements are shown explicitly before any revised schedule is recomputed.

07 — RECOMPUTE THE SCHEDULE

After confirmation, PortOptimiser recomputes candidate revised berth schedules.

The optimisation model applies the confirmed instruction together with the current schedule and modelled terminal constraints.

08 — COMPARE REVISED SCHEDULES

Compare the revised schedules.

Review what changes for Vessel B, Vessel D and berth use under each candidate revised schedule.

All timings and candidate schedules shown here are illustrative. A partner PoC would use the terminal’s actual layout, constraints and rescheduling priorities.
09 — HUMAN DECISION

The model supports the rescheduling decision; the scheduler makes it.

PortOptimiser identifies revised schedules that satisfy the represented constraints. The scheduler remains responsible for selecting the operational response.
