HUMAN APPROVAL

Scheduler selects the recovery plan.

PortOptimiser supports the decision. It does not replace the decision-maker.

01 — BASELINE

A feasible operating plan.

Vessels, berths and resources are aligned. Nothing needs to change.

02 — DISRUPTION

Then Vessel B is delayed four hours.

The ETA changes. The operating plan now has to absorb the consequences.

03 — KNOCK-ON EFFECTS

A disruption changes more than one time.

Berth availability, vessel sequence and resource timing can all become coupled.

04 — RECOVERY DECISION

Several recovery actions may be possible.

Each one can be feasible while creating different operational trade-offs.

05 — OPTIMISATION

PortOptimiser searches the recovery space.

Current state, disruption, operational constraints and priorities are evaluated together.

06 — CONVERSATIONAL INTERFACE

Ask the question the way a scheduler thinks.

The LLM helps express scenarios and explain results. The optimisation engine determines feasible alternatives.

07 — TRADE-OFFS

Compare options, not just answers.

Different feasible plans can favour delay reduction, schedule stability or resource continuity differently.

Illustrative indicators only. Partner KPIs would be validated during the PoC.
08 — HUMAN IN CONTROL

The scheduler decides.

AI assists. Optimisation verifies. Human judgement remains the final authority.
